# Changelog

All changes to the Bytepay governance deliverables are recorded here. Dates are ISO 8601.

## 2026-11-26

### Added
- Deliverable 05: Main project report (PDF). Covers initiation, organisational and system profile, decision model, six-stage workflow, risk assessment and treatment.
- Deliverable 03: Departmental roles and responsibilities (PDF). Responsibility matrix across eleven departments and parties.
- Deliverable 04: Project summary (PDF, 4 pages), executive summary of the assessment.
- Deliverable 1a: Editable risk register workbook (XLSX, v2).

### Changed
- Deliverable 01: Risk register replaced with v2, which adds residual risk scores and corrects duplicate department entries in the controls table. The filename is unchanged, so existing links continue to work.

### Pending

### Added
- Deliverable 01: Risk assessment register with treatment strategies (PDF v1). Six risks (101 to 106): customer privacy and data protection, false positives, false negatives, algorithmic bias, inadequate human oversight, and vendor accountability, with proposed controls, control types, responsible departments and references to NIST AI RMF 1.0 and NDPA 2023.
